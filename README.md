### Study Focus

The study investigates how school disciplinary procedures influence learner behaviour in selected high schools in Bulawayo Central District. It places particular emphasis on:

- disciplinary procedures used by schools
- learners' perceptions of those procedures
- the effectiveness of the procedures in enhancing positive learner behaviour
- challenges in implementation
- strategies for improvement

### Research Objectives

i. To identify the disciplinary procedures used to manage learner behaviour in selected high schools in Bulawayo Central District.
ii. To examine learners' perceptions of disciplinary procedures used in the school.
iii. To assess the effectiveness of school disciplinary procedures in enhancing positive learner behaviour.
iv. To examine the challenges associated with implementing school disciplinary procedures.
v. To proffer strategies for improving school disciplinary procedures necessary to promote positive learner behaviour.
